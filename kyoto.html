DISCOVER KYOTO

A city where the past lives on.

Kyoto is a city that beautifully preserves the traditions and cultural heritage of Japan. Once the country's capital, it is famous for its historic temples, traditional wooden houses and peaceful gardens.

From walking beneath the famous red gates of Fushimi Inari to exploring the quiet streets of Gion, Kyoto offers a memorable experience for every traveller.

EXPLORE THE CITY

Places you shouldn't miss.

Discover the temples, gardens and landmarks that make Kyoto truly special.

Fushimi Inari Shrine

Walk through thousands of vibrant red torii gates along beautiful forest trails at one of Japan's most famous shrines.

Kinkaku-ji

Also known as the Golden Pavilion, this beautiful Zen temple is famous for its golden exterior and peaceful reflection pond.

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Experience the peaceful atmosphere of towering bamboo trees and scenic walking paths in this beautiful natural setting.

Kiyomizu-dera

Visit this historic Buddhist temple, known for its impressive wooden terrace and scenic views over Kyoto.

A TASTE OF JAPAN

Discover the flavours of Kyoto.

Japanese cuisine is known for its balance, freshness and attention to detail. Kyoto offers traditional flavours alongside popular Japanese favourites.

Ramen

A comforting noodle soup served with a rich broth and different toppings.

Sushi

A classic Japanese dish combining seasoned rice with seafood or vegetables.

Matcha

Finely ground green tea traditionally enjoyed in Japanese tea ceremonies.

Yudofu

A traditional Kyoto dish featuring tofu gently simmered in a light broth.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

When should you visit Kyoto?

Each season transforms Kyoto into a different experience, from cherry blossoms to autumn colours.

Spring

MARCH – MAY

Famous cherry blossoms, pleasant weather and beautiful gardens.

Summer

JUNE – AUGUST

Lively festivals, green landscapes and warm, humid weather.

Autumn

SEPTEMBER – NOVEMBER

Stunning red and golden leaves, cooler weather and scenic temples.

Winter

DECEMBER – FEBRUARY

Quiet streets, chilly mornings and peaceful winter scenery.
