Venice

A city of winding canals, timeless architecture and unforgettable moments on the water.

The floating city

Where every street leads to a story.

Built across a collection of small islands in northeastern Italy, Venice is one of the world's most extraordinary cities. Instead of ordinary roads, its historic centre is shaped by canals, bridges and narrow walkways.

From quiet mornings beside the water to lively evenings in historic squares, Venice offers an atmosphere unlike anywhere else.

Language Italian
Currency Euro (€)

Places to discover

Venice, one beautiful corner at a time.

Explore the landmarks and hidden corners that make this city so memorable.

Grand Canal

The city's main waterway, lined with historic buildings and beautiful bridges.

St. Mark's Square

A remarkable public square surrounded by historic architecture and lively cafés.

Rialto Bridge

One of Venice's most recognised bridges, offering beautiful views over the Grand Canal.

Gondola Ride

Experience the city from its waterways and discover quieter canals along the way.

A taste of Venice

Flavours worth travelling for.

Venetian food reflects its history, coastal location and traditional Italian cooking.

Bigoli

A traditional thick Venetian pasta, often served with rich sauces.

Seafood

Fresh seafood inspired by the city's connection to the Adriatic Sea.

Cicchetti

Small Venetian bites traditionally enjoyed in local wine bars.

Tiramisu

A famous Italian dessert made with coffee, cream and cocoa.

Culture & experiences

A city that moves at its own pace.

Venice is known for its artistic heritage, historic architecture, traditional crafts and beautiful waterways.

Discover the city's glassmaking traditions on Murano Island, explore the colourful streets of Burano, or simply spend an afternoon walking through its quieter neighbourhoods.

Every bridge, courtyard and canal offers another reason to slow down and explore.

Plan your visit

Venice through the seasons.

Every season brings a different atmosphere to the city.

Spring

March – May

Mild weather and pleasant conditions for walking around the city.

Summer

June – August

Long sunny days, lively streets and a busy tourist season.

Autumn

September – November

Cooler temperatures and a calmer atmosphere after summer.

Winter

December – February

Quiet canals, colder weather and a unique winter atmosphere.
