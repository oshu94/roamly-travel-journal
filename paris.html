DISCOVER PARIS

Where every street tells a story.

Paris is one of the world's most loved travel destinations, known for its artistic heritage, historic architecture and romantic atmosphere.

From the iconic Eiffel Tower to the quiet streets of Montmartre, every corner of the city offers something worth discovering. Whether you enjoy art, food, fashion or history, Paris has something for everyone.

EXPLORE THE CITY

Places you shouldn't miss.

Discover the landmarks and neighbourhoods that make Paris unforgettable.

The Eiffel Tower

The most recognisable symbol of Paris. Enjoy beautiful views of the city from this iconic landmark.

The Louvre Museum

Explore one of the world's most famous museums, home to remarkable works of art and history.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Admire the remarkable Gothic architecture and rich history of this famous cathedral.

Montmartre

Wander through artistic streets, discover charming cafés and experience a different side of Paris.

A TASTE OF FRANCE

A little taste of Paris.

French cuisine is an important part of the Parisian experience. From bakery favourites to sweet treats, there is always something delicious to discover.

Croissant

A buttery, flaky pastry often enjoyed with morning coffee.

Macarons

Delicate French almond cookies with colourful fillings.

Baguette

The classic French bread found in bakeries across the city.

Crêpes

Thin French pancakes served with sweet or savoury fillings.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

When should you visit Paris?

Every season brings a different atmosphere and a new way to experience the city.

Spring

MARCH – MAY

Pleasant weather, blooming gardens and beautiful outdoor walks.

Summer

JUNE – AUGUST

Longer days, outdoor events and lively streets across the city.

Autumn

SEPTEMBER – NOVEMBER

Colourful leaves, cooler weather and a cosy city atmosphere.

Winter

DECEMBER – FEBRUARY

Chilly days, festive decorations and charming winter cafés.
