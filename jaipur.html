Rajasthan · India

Jaipur

A royal city of magnificent palaces, vibrant markets, historic forts and timeless Rajasthani traditions.

The Pink City

Where royal heritage meets vibrant culture.

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is a city filled with architectural beauty, colourful streets and centuries of royal history.

Known for its distinctive pink buildings, grand forts, traditional handicrafts and delicious local cuisine, Jaipur offers a wonderful glimpse into India's heritage.

Country India
State Rajasthan
Language Hindi
Currency Indian Rupee
Best time October – March

Places to discover

Stories carved in stone and colour.

Discover the landmarks that reveal Jaipur's royal past and architectural brilliance.

Hawa Mahal

The iconic Palace of Winds, famous for its detailed pink sandstone facade and hundreds of small windows.

Amber Fort

A magnificent hilltop fort known for its grand courtyards, artistic interiors and impressive architecture.

Jal Mahal

A beautiful palace appearing to float in the middle of Man Sagar Lake.

City Palace

A royal complex featuring courtyards, museums, traditional decorations and historic architecture.

A taste of Rajasthan

Flavours full of tradition.

Jaipur's food brings together rich flavours, traditional recipes and local ingredients.

Dal Baati Churma

A traditional Rajasthani combination of baked wheat balls, lentils and sweet churma.

Gatte ki Sabzi

A popular Rajasthani curry made with gram flour dumplings.

Pyaaz Kachori

A crispy pastry filled with a flavourful spiced onion mixture.

Ghewar

A traditional Rajasthani sweet with a delicate honeycomb-like texture.

Culture & experiences

Traditions that bring the city to life.

Jaipur is known for its colourful festivals, traditional Rajasthani music, folk dances and vibrant cultural celebrations.

Explore local markets filled with block-printed textiles, blue pottery, jewellery and handmade crafts.

Experience the warmth of Rajasthani hospitality and discover the traditions that continue to shape everyday life in the city.
